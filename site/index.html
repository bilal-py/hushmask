Tell the AI everything except who you are.

hushmask hides names, emails, phone numbers, IBANs and other personal details before your text reaches an AI, then puts them back in the answer. It runs on your machine, and any app that talks to an OpenAI-style API can use it by changing one URL.

Try it in your browser Run it yourself

Your prompts don't disappear.

Everything you paste into an AI is stored on someone else's server. Depending on the provider and plan, people may read it and it may train the next model. hushmask makes sure the names, numbers and addresses were never in it.

  • Kept in logs
    Prompts are stored, often for weeks.
  • Read by people
    Reviews for safety and quality can include your text.
  • Used for training
    On some plans, unless you opt out.

What to hide

Always hide

    Names nobody listed (like "Mia Schulz" in the support ticket) need the local names model that comes with hushmask; patterns alone can't know a word is a name. In this demo, add them to "Always hide" and watch them disappear.

    Your text

    What the AI receives

    people IBAN, card and ID numbers email and phone addresses and places

    Use cloud AI on the text you actually have.

    The useful text is usually the personal kind. hushmask lets you hand it over without handing over the people in it.

    One request, four stops.

    hushmask sits between your app and the AI and pretends to be the AI. Your app changes one setting, the base URL, and everything else stays the same.

    
          
    
          
    
        
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    Hides who. Keeps what.
    
      
    Hiding everything is easy and useless: an AI that can't read the request can't help with it. hushmask hides the people and keeps the subject, the dates, the amounts and the words that matter.
    
      
    
        
    People
    Names from your "always hide" list, and anyone else's from a local names model. "Mr Becker" links to "Jonas Becker", so the AI knows it's the same person.
    
        
    Ways to reach you
    Email addresses, including ones like anna.müller@…, and phone numbers in international and national formats.
    
        
    Numbers that identify you
    IBANs and card numbers, checked with their checksums so random numbers are left alone, and account numbers next to a label. Indian ID formats too.
    
        
    Where you live
    Street addresses as German, Dutch, French, Italian, Spanish, US, UK and Australian write them, plus anything the names model finds. Cities in the strict preset only.
    
      
    
      
    Same value, same token, every time. A whole conversation stays consistent, the AI can refer back to "PERSON_4aee58", and the provider's prompt cache keeps working.

      Rules it doesn't bend.
      
      
      
        
      Fails closed
      After masking, the text is scanned again. If anything detectable is still visible, the request is refused instead of sent.
      
        
      Real values stay in memory
      The map from tokens to values lives for the request. Logs and stats hold counts, never values.
      
        
      Won't send what it can't mask
      Images, audio and files are refused unless you allow them for an app, because there's no way to hide what's in them.
      
        
      The dashboard is yours alone
      It only answers on localhost and needs a header other websites can't add, so a page in another tab can't read your apps.
      
      
      
    
      
  
      

  
      
    
      
      
      Good to know
      
      
      
        
      Is this anonymization?
      No. It hides who you are from the AI provider. Dates, amounts, places and the subject stay visible on purpose, and in combination they can still say a lot. If you need data nobody could ever link back to a person, this isn't that.
      
        
      Which AI providers work?
      Anything that speaks the OpenAI chat-completions API: OpenAI, Gemini's OpenAI-compatible endpoint, Ollama, OpenRouter, Groq, LM Studio and others. Each app in the dashboard can use a different one.
      
        
      Does it work with streaming?
      Your app can ask for a stream and it will work, but hushmask waits for the whole answer before passing it on. A token split across two chunks couldn't be put back reliably.
      
        
      What if the AI changes a token?
      Tokens are restored even if the model changes their case. A token the AI invents, or one hushmask never sent, is left as it is and counted, so you can see it in the dashboard.
      
        
      Does it need the internet?
      Only to reach the AI you choose, and once to download the names model. With Ollama, everything stays on your machine.
      
        
      Does it understand German?
      Yes. Patterns work on any language, the names model is multilingual (90% of personal values hidden on German everyday text), and German labels like "Kontonummer" are recognised.
